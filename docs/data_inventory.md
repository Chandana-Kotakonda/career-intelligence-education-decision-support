# Data Inventory

This document records the datasets used or planned for the
Career Intelligence & Education Decision Support System.

| Dataset | Purpose | Source | Format | Status |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| O*NET Occupations | Career and occupation knowledge | O*NET | CSV | Collected & Validated |
| O*NET Skills | Skill requirements for occupations | O*NET | CSV | Collected & Validated |
| O*NET Education | Education requirements for occupations | O*NET | CSV | Collected & Validated |
| Job Descriptions | NLP and job-market analysis | Public dataset | CSV | Collected & Validated |
| AISHE | Higher education and programme information | AISHE | XLSX | Collected & Validated |
| Scholarships | Scholarship matching | NSP | CSV/Manual | Removed from scope |
| Exams | Exam intelligence | Official sources | CSV | Planned |
| Careers | Career taxonomy | Curated | CSV | Planned |
| Pathways | Education pathways | Curated | CSV | Planned |
